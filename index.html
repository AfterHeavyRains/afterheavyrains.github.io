About

I am a doctoral researcher at Tongji University. My research interests lie at the intersection of physical modeling and machine learning, with a focus on physics-consistent representations, multiphysics coupling, and world models for embodied intelligence.

My research background includes acoustic emission analysis, time-series signal processing, and intelligent condition monitoring. I am interested in connecting these foundations with learning-based models of physical dynamics.
